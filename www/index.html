Software updates

    Restart reminders

    Restarting clears spyware and other bad code that hides only in the phone’s memory, and installs waiting updates. Security agencies advise restarting regularly. Apps aren’t allowed to restart a phone by themselves, so GuardYourPhone reminds you at 5:00 am and 7:00 pm every day.

    How to restart
    • Most Android phones: press and hold the power button (or power + volume up on newer Samsung and Pixel phones), then tap Restart.
    • iPhone: press and hold the side button and either volume button, slide to power off, wait 30 seconds, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.

    Protection against spyware and snooping

    Zero-click attacks (like the LANDFALL spyware sent as pictures over WhatsApp), “man-in-the-middle” snooping on your internet, and fake mobile towers that grab your phone’s identity (IMSI and IMEI). GuardYourPhone checks what it can; tick off the rest as you do them.

      Processors and firmware

      The application processor runs Android and your apps. The baseband (modem) processor runs the mobile radio with its own firmware, which apps can’t inspect directly; GuardYourPhone shows its firmware version so you can check it’s up to date.

      Apps with powerful access

      Apps that can read your screen, your notifications or texts, control the phone, or that weren’t installed from an app store. Most are fine; remove any you don’t recognise.

      Mobile network

      Checks which network your phone is using. 2G is old and its encryption is weak; fake mobile towers (“IMSI catchers”) often force phones down to 2G.

      Phone service codes

      Codes you type into the Phone dialler to verify your phone and check if your calls are being diverted. Tap a code to copy it, then paste it into the keypad. These are for Android / Samsung; on an iPhone only *#06# works.

      • IMEI & serial. Check it at samsung.com/support/imei — it must match your model. Also use it to report a lost or stolen phone.
      • Firmware (AP / CP / CSC). Confirms a genuine build and your region. A fake phone shows gibberish or nothing.
      • Hardware test menu. A real Samsung opens a full screen / sensor test; a counterfeit usually can’t. Just exit when done.
      • Call-forwarding check. Shows if your calls, texts or data are being diverted (a SIM-swap trick). It does not detect spyware.
      • Unreachable forwarding. The number used when your phone is off — should be your voicemail, not a strange number.
      • Cancel all forwarding. Clears any diversion you find. Safe for normal use.

      Never dial a code you don’t recognise.

      Some codes wipe the phone instantly with no warning (for example *2767*3855# or *#*#7780#*#*). Never enter a code someone messages you or a website tells you to, and never tap a tel: link from a stranger.

      No dial code can detect modern spyware — anyone claiming a “secret code” shows if you’re hacked is wrong. These codes verify your phone; the checks above protect it.
