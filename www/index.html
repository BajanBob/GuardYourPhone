Software updates

    Restart reminders

    Restarting clears spyware and other bad code that hides only in the phone’s memory, and installs waiting updates. Security agencies advise restarting regularly. Apps aren’t allowed to restart a phone by themselves, so GuardYourPhone reminds you at 5:00 am and 7:00 pm every day.

    How to restart
    • Most Android phones: press and hold the power button (or power + volume up on newer Samsung and Pixel phones), then tap Restart.
    • iPhone: press and hold the side button and either volume button, slide to power off, wait 30 seconds, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.

    Protection against spyware and snooping

    Zero-click attacks (like the LANDFALL spyware sent as pictures over WhatsApp), “man-in-the-middle” snooping on your internet, and fake mobile towers that grab your phone’s identity (IMSI and IMEI). GuardYourPhone checks what it can; tick off the rest as you do them.

      Processors and firmware

      The application processor runs Android and your apps. The baseband (modem) processor runs the mobile radio with its own firmware, which apps can’t inspect directly; GuardYourPhone shows its firmware version so you can check it’s up to date.

      Apps with powerful access

      Mobile network
